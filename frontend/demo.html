The challenge

SPHEREx revisits the same sky over time.

To find movers and variables, people need to compare visits. WiseView did this for WISE — SPHEREx still needed a clear blink tool built on IRSA data.

What we built

Blinkit

  • Flip / blink real SPHEREx visits (D1–D6)
  • Color, minus-median, difference, stack
  • Gaia DR3 markers and motion proof
  • Six Eyes — deep stack per detector
  • Official targets + any RA/Dec · shareable URLs

Demo flow (~30 seconds)

Try it live

  • Open Barnard's Star on blinkit.study
  • Play the flipbook across visits
  • Check Difference or Color
  • Open Six Eyes for all six detectors

Why it matters

Human eyes on time-domain SPHEREx

Blinkit makes change obvious without claiming a discovery. Real NASA pixels, honest flags, and proofs against Gaia — for students, citizens, and scientists as SPHEREx keeps mapping the sky.
