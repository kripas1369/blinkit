## Script (say this)

> Hi — we’re **Not42**, and this is **Blinkit**.  
> SPHEREx revisits the same sky over time. Blinkit turns those visits into a **flipbook**, so you can blink and see what moved or changed.  
> Pick an official target like **Barnard's Star**, flip through detectors **D1–D6**, check **difference** and **motion proof** against Gaia, and open **Six Eyes** for a deep stack and coarse spectrum.  
> Real NASA IRSA data. Share any view with a link. That’s Blinkit.
